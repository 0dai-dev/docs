# Promotion Policy

Promote experience into durable knowledge only when it is repeated, scoped, and validated.

## Promotion Thresholds

- repeat count >= 3 for the same failure or success pattern
- measurable impact on CI, review quality, rework, or recovery time
- validated by at least one of: CI, human review, repeated success, or incident analysis
- clear scope by stack, paths, or tools
- no contradiction with existing rules or packs

## Promotion Targets

- `rule`: permanent instruction or constraint
- `skill`: repeatable workflow or recipe
- `playbook`: coordinated human + agent process
- `anti-pattern`: recurring mistake to avoid

## Rejection Signals

- one-off anecdote
- unclear scope
- low confidence evidence
- duplicates existing knowledge
- depends on personal preference only
